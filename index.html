My Dance Journey

A decade of dedication, rhythm, and self-discovery.

Rhythm of the Soul

The journey began over a decade ago with the first namaskaram. What started as curiosity soon blossomed into a profound passion for the rich storytelling and disciplined movement of Bharatanatyam.

Through countless hours of practice, blisters from rhythmic stamping (tattadavus), and the mastery of intricate mudras, this path has instilled deep devotion, patience, and a connection to our ancient cultural heritage.

The Arangetram marks not an end, but the sacred beginning of a lifelong artistic voyage.

About The Guru

Smt. Lakshmi Narayan

Founder & Artistic Director

Under the masterful guidance of Guru Smt. Lakshmi Narayan, generations of students have discovered the divine art of Bharatanatyam. With over 30 years of performing and teaching experience, her approach perfectly balances rigid traditional grammar with deeply expressive choreography.

Her dedication to preserving the authentic South Indian temple purity while presenting it to modern audiences makes every student's learning journey an immersive cultural experience.

Arathi School of Dance

A Sanctuary of Tradition

Arathi School of Dance has been a cultural pillar in our community for two decades. The school emphasizes not just the physical vocabulary of dance, but the profound spiritual philosophy embedded within it.

From studying the Natya Shastra to rhythmic theory (Tala), the holistic curriculum shapes well-rounded artists who carry the torch of classical Indian heritage.
